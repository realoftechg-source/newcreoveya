OBS setup steps
  1. Download and open OBS Studio (free, obsproject.com).
  2. Under Sources, click + → Browser, and paste your Creoveya AI Live Studio URL above.
  3. Set the Browser Source size to match your resolution (e.g. 1280x720).
  4. Keep this dashboard's AI Studio tab open — your camera preview shows up in OBS right away.
  5. Click Start Streaming in AI Studio — OBS switches to your AI output automatically.
  6. In OBS, click Start Virtual Camera.
Use it anywhere

Once OBS's Virtual Camera is running, select "OBS Virtual Camera" as your webcam in any app:

  • Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams
  • WhatsApp Desktop / Web video calls
  • Discord voice channel video
  • TikTok LIVE Studio, Twitch, YouTube Live
