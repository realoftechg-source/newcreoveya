Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Payments Are Manually Reviewed

All plan purchases on {{ SITE_NAME }} are submitted with a payment receipt and reviewed by our team before credits are added to your account. If your payment is rejected (for example, due to an unclear or invalid receipt), no credits are issued and no charge is retained by us for that submission — you're welcome to resubmit with a valid receipt.

2. Approved Payments

Once a payment is approved and credits have been added to your account, that purchase is final and non-refundable, except as described below. This is because credits become immediately available for use (streaming, AI processing) upon approval, and usage cost is incurred by us in real time as you stream.

3. Unused Credits

If you have unused, unexpired credits and wish to close your account, contact us via our Contact Us page. Refund requests for unused credit balances are considered on a case-by-case basis and are not guaranteed, particularly where significant time has passed since purchase or where any portion of the credits has already been used.

4. Technical Issues

If a documented, platform-side technical failure (not caused by your device, network, or browser) prevents you from using credits you've purchased, contact support with details of the issue. We will review each case individually and may issue replacement credits or, where appropriate, a partial refund at our discretion.

5. Cryptocurrency Payments

Cryptocurrency transactions are irreversible by nature. We are not able to reverse or recall a crypto payment once sent to the wallet address we provide. Please double-check the wallet address, network, and amount carefully before sending payment.

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund under the circumstances above, contact us via our Contact Us page with your account username, the transaction date, and a description of the issue. We aim to respond to all refund inquiries within a reasonable timeframe.
